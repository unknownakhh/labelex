PRODUCT 02 / 03

Heat shrink
sleeves.

Full-body 360° decoration that conforms to any container shape — square, contoured, tapered, multi-curved. Three film options. The most premium-feeling label format we manufacture.

  • 360° wrap covers the entire container — no visible seam, no exposed material
  • Wraps any shape: square, oval, hourglass, tapered, multi-tier
  • Tamper-evident options for pharma, dairy, supplements
  • Up to 70% shrink ratio for highly contoured containers

PRODUCT 03 / 03

Hot melt
glue labels.

High-speed roll-fed labelling for cylindrical containers. The lowest unit cost we manufacture. Built for FMCG and beverage production lines running thousands of units per hour.

  • Lowest cost per unit at high volumes — engineered for production line economics
  • Optimised for cylindrical containers: glass bottles, PET, aluminium cans, jars
  • Compatible with major roll-fed labelling machinery (Krones, Sidel, KHS, Sacmi)
  • Recyclability-friendly when paired with appropriate adhesive systems
