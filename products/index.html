Heat shrink
sleeves.

Full body 360 degree decoration that conforms to any container shape — square, contoured, tapered and multi-curved. Three film options. Shrink sleeve labels for bottles, jars and complex container shapes across any South African industry.

  • Full 360 degree coverage with no visible seam and no exposed container surface
  • Suitable for square, oval, hourglass, tapered and multi-tier container shapes
  • Tamper-evident for pharmaceutical, dairy and supplement products
  • Up to 70 percent shrink ratio for highly contoured containers

Hot melt
glue labels.

High speed roll-fed labelling for cylindrical containers. Hot melt glue labels are the format of choice for FMCG and beverage producers running high volume production lines across South Africa. Consistent output, clean bond and the lowest cost per unit at scale.

  • Lowest cost per unit at high volumes making it the right choice for production line economics
  • Optimised for cylindrical containers including glass bottles, PET, aluminium cans and jars
  • Compatible with major roll-fed labelling equipment used across South African production facilities
  • Paired with adhesive systems that support recyclability where required

How it works

From your brief to your door in four steps.

01 · BRIEF

Send the spec.

Send us your artwork, quantities, what you are labelling and your deadline. We will have a quote back to you within 24 hours.

Approve your digital proof and pay a 50% deposit. Your 14 to 21 day lead time begins from the date of payment.
