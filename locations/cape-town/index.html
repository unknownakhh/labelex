Cape Town · Western Cape Industries

Industries we serve in Cape Town and the Western Cape.

Wine and Premium Beverage

The Cape Winelands stretch across Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, Paarl, Constantia, Robertson, Swartland, Hemel-en-Aarde and Elgin: more than 300 wine estates producing for domestic retail and export markets across Europe, Asia and the United States. South Africa exports wine to over 100 countries, and the Western Cape produces roughly 80% of that volume. Wine labels face a unique set of performance demands. They must bond reliably to glass in cold cellar conditions, survive condensation and humidity through distribution and storage, and hold print quality through ice bucket immersion and handling at the point of sale. Gloss, matte and textured finishes all affect how premium the bottle feels on shelf, so the label is a commercial decision as much as a compliance one. Labelex prints wine-ready self adhesive labels on moisture-resistant substrates with cold-surface adhesives suited to the conditions of Western Cape wine production and export.

Craft Beer and Independent Breweries

Cape Town has developed one of the most concentrated independent brewery communities on the African continent, with craft beer operations active in Woodstock, Salt River, Observatory, Newlands, Muizenberg, Constantia and across the Northern and Southern Suburbs. Independent craft beer brands compete on shelf against each other and against major commercial labels, so the label becomes one of the primary differentiation tools. Craft beer labels must survive refrigerated storage, cold glass bottle surfaces at application, and ice-water environments in retail chillers and hospitality settings. Heat shrink sleeves give craft brands 360-degree, full-circumference label presence. Self adhesive PP and BOPP labels suit producers who apply labels on smaller semi-automated lines. Labelex prints both formats and advises on the correct approach based on your bottling setup and volume.

Seafood Processing and Cold Chain Export

Cape Town is the operational hub of South Africa's fishing and seafood processing industry. Major seafood businesses including Sea Harvest, I&J and the Oceana Group (Lucky Star) process and export significant volumes of frozen and fresh seafood through Cape Town and the Western Cape coast. Frozen seafood labels face the most demanding storage conditions of any label application: sub-zero freezer temperatures, condensation from temperature cycling during cold chain distribution, and wet handling at processing and retail stages. Standard adhesive labels fail in frozen storage. The adhesive loses tack, the substrate curls, and the label detaches or becomes illegible. Labelex produces waterproof, cold-chain-rated labels on substrates and adhesive formulations built to hold bond and print integrity at sub-zero temperatures. If your product lives in a freezer, your label needs to be built for it.

Fresh Produce and Export Agriculture

The Western Cape is South Africa's primary fresh produce export region. Table grapes from the Hex River Valley, citrus from the Swartland and Olifants River, stone fruit from Ceres, and apples and pears from Elgin and Grabouw move through the Port of Cape Town to export markets across Europe, Asia and the Middle East. Export produce labelling has specific requirements: PLU stickers for international retail, traceability codes for food safety compliance, country of origin marking, and in some cases destination-country language requirements for regulated categories. Labelex prints produce labels and export compliance stickers for Western Cape packhouses and export agents. Fast print turnaround matters in seasonal export, and we understand the pace of a harvest.

Natural Cosmetics and Organic Beauty

Cape Town and the Western Cape have a distinctive and growing natural beauty sector, built on the region's unique botanical heritage: rooibos from the Cederberg, buchu from the Western Cape mountains and fynbos-derived extracts. Brands in this space sell premium positioning to both domestic and international consumers, so the label has to reflect the quality of what is inside the packaging. Cosmetic labels must comply with SAHPRA cosmetic product labelling requirements, survive daily moisture and humidity exposure in bathroom environments, and print accurately and consistently across production runs to protect brand integrity. Labelex prints cosmetic labels on substrates including clear BOPP for a premium no-label look, pearlescent for a luxury finish, and PP for durability and vibrant colour reproduction.

Food Manufacturers and FMCG

Epping Industria and Montague Gardens, Cape Town's two largest industrial estates, house a significant concentration of food processing, FMCG manufacturing, and distribution businesses. Woolworths Holdings and The Foschini Group are headquartered in Cape Town — both sourcing labelled products from Western Cape manufacturers at scale. Food labels in this sector must meet R638 food labelling regulations, achieve consistent colour and barcode print quality across long production runs, and maintain adhesion through refrigerated storage and distribution. Labelex supplies self adhesive labels to food producers, repackagers, and FMCG brands operating out of Cape Town's industrial zones.

Yes. Labelex prints craft beer labels in both self adhesive and heat shrink sleeve formats. Self adhesive labels on PP or BOPP are suited to small and medium-run craft breweries applying labels on semi-automated lines. What labels are suitable for frozen seafood products?+

Frozen seafood labels require waterproof substrates and low-temperature adhesives that maintain tack at sub-zero temperatures. Standard pressure-sensitive labels lose adhesion in freezer conditions, the adhesive becomes brittle and the bond fails. Labelex produces cold-chain-rated labels on PP with low-temp adhesive formulations designed for frozen storage applications. Describe your product, packaging format and storage temperature, and we will recommend the correct substrate and adhesive combination before production begins.
