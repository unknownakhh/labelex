Pretoria · Tshwane Industries

Industries we serve in Pretoria.

Automotive Manufacturing

Pretoria's Rosslyn corridor is one of South Africa's most significant automotive manufacturing hubs. BMW South Africa has operated its Rosslyn assembly plant since 1968 and remains one of the largest vehicle manufacturers on the African continent. Nissan South Africa also manufactures from Rosslyn, contributing to a dense cluster of Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive component suppliers in the Pretoria North and Rosslyn industrial areas. Label requirements in automotive manufacturing are demanding: component identification labels must withstand oils, solvents, and temperature variation across production and storage environments. VIN and compliance labels must meet precise format and durability standards. Safety and warning labels require specific substrate and adhesive combinations to ensure permanent bonding under industrial conditions. Labelex manufactures durable self adhesive labels on substrates suited to automotive applications and works with component suppliers across the Rosslyn corridor.

Government and Parastatal Organisations

As the executive capital of South Africa, Pretoria houses a concentration of government departments, state-owned enterprises, and parastatals that no other South African city can match. Entities including the CSIR, SABS, the Department of Public Works, and dozens of national government departments are headquartered in Tshwane. Public sector label requirements span asset management stickers, document control labels, security identification tags, and branded marking for state property. Government procurement has specific requirements around label durability, print quality, and traceability — requirements Labelex understands and meets. We produce labels for public sector buyers operating within standard government procurement frameworks.

Pharmaceutical and Research

The CSIR — Council for Scientific and Industrial Research — is headquartered in Pretoria and represents one of the largest scientific research institutions on the African continent. The broader Tshwane area hosts pharmaceutical manufacturers, contract research organisations, and laboratory facilities with labelling requirements governed by SAHPRA regulations and good manufacturing practice (GMP) standards. Laboratory identification labels, product batch stickers, serialisation labels, and compliant pharmaceutical packaging labels require precision printing on appropriate substrates with verifiable colour consistency across production runs. Labelex manufactures labels for the pharmaceutical and research sector and understands the compliance context in which these products are applied.

Food and Beverage Producers

Pretoria and the Centurion industrial corridor support a substantial food and beverage manufacturing base. Fresh produce packagers, beverage brands, and processed food manufacturers across Tshwane require labelling that complies with the South African Department of Health's R638 food labelling regulations, survives cold chain storage, and presents consistently on retail shelf. Labelex manufactures food-grade self adhesive labels on substrates including clear BOPP for a no-label look on glass and PET packaging, white PP for high-opacity product branding, and waterproof materials for products stored in refrigerated or humid environments.

Retail, Distribution and FMCG

Menlyn, Centurion, and Pretoria East are home to major retail operations, distribution centres, and FMCG brand offices serving the Tshwane catchment. Product labelling for the retail channel demands consistent colour reproduction across print runs, barcode readability that passes point-of-sale scanning requirements, and substrate durability across warehousing, transit, and in-store handling. Labelex works with product brands, importers, repackagers, and distributors across Pretoria supplying adhesive product labels and promotional stickers to retail-ready standards. We accommodate both short run re-label jobs and high-volume ongoing supply agreements.

Education, Universities and Research Institutions

Pretoria is South Africa's academic and research capital. The University of Pretoria, the Tshwane University of Technology, and the University of South Africa (UNISA) — the largest distance-education institution on the continent, headquartered in the city — sit alongside national research bodies including the CSIR and the Human Sciences Research Council. Together they form one of the densest concentrations of institutional buyers in the country. Label requirements across this sector are steady and repeat-driven: asset identification and inventory tags for IT equipment, furniture, and laboratory instruments; specimen and sample labels for research and teaching laboratories; library and archive labelling; and durable barcode stickers for facilities and stores management. These buyers procure through formal tender and quotation processes and value a supplier who holds consistent specification across repeat orders. Labelex manufactures durable asset, laboratory, and identification labels for Pretoria's universities, research councils, and institutional facilities, quoted to standard procurement requirements.

Pretoria · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you manufacture labels in Pretoria?+

Labelex does not operate a Pretoria factory — we manufacture from our Gauteng facility in Sandton and deliver directly to Pretoria and all Tshwane suburbs. Being Gauteng-based means Pretoria sits within our primary delivery corridor, giving customers one of the shortest supplier-to-door distances available in the SA label manufacturing market. All quoting, production, and delivery is handled from a single contact point.

What is the minimum order for Pretoria customers?+

Our minimum order value is R3,000 per product type. There is no location-based pricing difference — the same minimum applies whether you are ordering from Pretoria, Durban, or Cape Town. At R3,000, most customers receive a meaningful quantity of labels that covers their immediate needs with stock held for the next reorder cycle. High-volume recurring customers can discuss tiered pricing as run length increases.

How long does delivery to Pretoria take after production?+

Pretoria falls within our primary Gauteng delivery zone. Standard delivery from our Sandton facility to Pretoria addresses is typically 1–2 business days after production is complete. Production lead time varies based on run size, substrate, and current capacity and is confirmed at the quoting stage. If you have a deadline, let us know at the time of enquiry and we will advise on feasibility.

Can you produce labels for automotive component manufacturers in Rosslyn?+

Yes. We manufacture durable self adhesive labels on substrates suited to automotive environments, including materials with resistance to oils, mild solvents, and elevated temperatures. Component identification, VIN compliance, safety warnings, and parts tracking labels are all within our production capability. If you supply into the Rosslyn automotive corridor or service BMW South Africa or Nissan South Africa as a Tier 1 or Tier 2 supplier, contact us to discuss your specific label specification and we will recommend the appropriate substrate and adhesive combination.

Do you supply labels that comply with SAHPRA pharmaceutical regulations?+

Labelex manufactures labels that meet the print quality, substrate, and traceability requirements standard in pharmaceutical labelling environments. We produce on substrates appropriate for pharmaceutical packaging and can accommodate batch number printing, serialisation requirements, and multi-colour regulated copy. We are a label manufacturer, not a pharmaceutical consultant — for regulatory advice specific to your product registration, consult your SAHPRA-registered compliance specialist. For the physical label itself, we manufacture to your approved artwork and specification.

What artwork format do you require for label printing?+

Supply your artwork as a print-ready PDF or Adobe Illustrator (AI) file at the correct label dimensions, including a 2mm bleed on all sides and a clearly marked die-cut line on a separate layer. All fonts must be outlined or embedded. Colour mode must be CMYK — not RGB. If your file does not meet these requirements, we will advise on what needs to be corrected before production proceeds. We do not offer in-house graphic design as a standard service.

What information do you need to quote my label order?+

To quote accurately we need five things: label dimensions (width and height in millimetres), the substrate or material if known, the number of print colours or whether it is full colour, the total quantity or estimated monthly volume, and the container or surface the label is applied to. If you are unsure about substrate or finish, send a description or a photograph of your product and we will recommend the correct specification. The more detail you provide up front, the faster the quote — most complete enquiries are quoted within one business day.
