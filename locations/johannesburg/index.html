Johannesburg · Industries Served

Industries we serve in Johannesburg.

FMCG and Food Manufacturing

Johannesburg is the FMCG capital of South Africa. Tiger Brands — one of Africa's largest branded goods manufacturers producing Jungle Oats, Koo, All Gold, and Black Cat — is headquartered in Johannesburg. AVI Limited, Premier FMCG, Clover Industries, and Coca-Cola Beverages SA all operate significant Johannesburg manufacturing and distribution footprints. The label requirements across this sector are as diverse as the product ranges themselves: product identification labels complying with R638 food labelling regulations, cold chain adhesive labels for refrigerated and frozen products, promotional and seasonal label variants, barcode labels meeting GS1 scanning standards, and primary packaging stickers across seven substrate options. Labelex manufactures for food and FMCG brands across Johannesburg's industrial zones — Aeroton, Amalgam, and the broader southern and western manufacturing belt — where production volumes are high and label supply consistency is non-negotiable.

Pharmaceutical and Health

Johannesburg and Midrand host a significant pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution cluster. Adcock Ingram, one of South Africa's largest JSE-listed pharmaceutical manufacturers and the producer of Panado, Compral, and Allergex, operates from Midrand. Numerous generic pharmaceutical manufacturers operate across the Johannesburg metropolitan area. Dis-Chem Pharmacies, headquartered in Midrand, sources own-label health and beauty products requiring consistent label supply. Pharmaceutical labels in this sector must comply with SAHPRA product labelling requirements, achieve consistent colour reproduction for regulatory compliance, and include accurate batch, serialisation, and expiry date print. Labelex manufactures pharmaceutical and health product labels for Johannesburg and Midrand operations, with substrates and print quality appropriate for regulated packaging environments.

Ekurhuleni Manufacturing Corridor

Ekurhuleni is the industrial backbone of South Africa. Spanning Boksburg, Germiston, Benoni, Springs, Kempton Park, and Edenvale, this corridor houses over 1,500 manufacturing operations — more manufacturing concentration in one geographic zone than anywhere else on the continent. The label requirements across Ekurhuleni are technically demanding and unusually diverse. Aerospace manufacturers including Denel Aerostructures in Kempton Park require precision component identification labels that meet international aviation standards. Automotive component suppliers — feeding assembly plants in Rosslyn, East London, and Port Elizabeth — need VIN stickers, safety warning labels, and parts identification stickers built to OEM specifications. Chemical manufacturers across the corridor need GHS-compliant hazardous goods labels with correct pictograms, signal words, and H-and-P-statement copy. Electronics and engineering businesses need durable asset tags and serialisation stickers that survive harsh manufacturing environments. Labelex manufactures across all of these requirements from Sandton — inside the Johannesburg metro and within easy delivery distance of every industrial node in the East Rand.

Logistics, Distribution and the OR Tambo Corridor

OR Tambo International Airport is Africa's busiest airport, handling approximately 21 million passengers annually and processing a significant volume of South Africa's air freight. The logistics and distribution infrastructure built around OR Tambo — spanning Kempton Park, Isando, and Spartan — represents one of the most active label-consuming environments in the country. Freight operators, courier companies, and third-party logistics providers require address labels, routing stickers, packing list holders, and barcode labels at high volume and on short notice. Distribution centres in Woodmead, Midrand, and the East Rand — serving retail chains and FMCG brands nationally — require product compliance stickers, pick-and-pack labels, and retail-ready packaging labels processed at distribution speed. Labelex supplies logistics and distribution operations across the OR Tambo corridor with adhesive labels manufactured to the volume and turnaround requirements of a fast-moving supply chain.

Retail and Consumer Goods

Johannesburg is South Africa's largest retail market by population and purchasing power. Major retail groups — Woolworths, Pick n Pay, Shoprite, Spar, Game, and Makro — all operate buying, distribution, and regional management operations from within the Johannesburg metro. The supply chain feeding these retailers includes hundreds of product manufacturers and packagers, many of whom source labels locally from Gauteng suppliers. Own-brand and private label products sold through Johannesburg retailers require consistent colour reproduction, clean barcode print, and substrate durability across ambient and refrigerated retail environments. Labelex works with product brands, contract packagers, and importers in Johannesburg supplying adhesive product labels and packaging stickers to the standards required for major retail distribution.

Corporate and Financial Services

Sandton, Rosebank, and the Johannesburg CBD house the largest concentration of corporate headquarters in Africa. The JSE, Standard Bank, FirstRand, Absa, Nedbank, Discovery Health, and hundreds of JSE-listed companies operate from within the Sandton node. Corporate label requirements in this sector span asset identification stickers for IT equipment, furniture, and infrastructure; branded labels for corporate communications and events; document control stickers for regulated industries; and security labels for high-value asset protection. Labelex supplies corporate and financial services clients across Sandton, Rosebank, and the Johannesburg CBD with professional adhesive labels at the quantity and quality appropriate for corporate procurement.

Do you manufacture pharmaceutical labels that comply with SAHPRA?+

Labelex manufactures labels on substrates and to print quality standards appropriate for pharmaceutical packaging environments. We produce batch, serialisation, and expiry date labels with consistent colour reproduction across production runs. We are a label manufacturer — not a regulatory consultant. Your SAHPRA-registered compliance specialist advises on what your label must say and how it must be formatted. We manufacture the physical label to your approved artwork, to the print and substrate standard required for pharmaceutical use.

Can you produce GHS-compliant chemical labels for Ekurhuleni manufacturers?+

Yes. GHS chemical labels require correct pictograms, signal words, and hazard and precautionary statements in the specified format. Labelex manufactures chemical labels on durable substrates suited to industrial environments — PVC and PP are the most common choices where resistance to moisture, chemicals, and abrasion is required. Supply your approved GHS-compliant artwork and we manufacture the label to specification. We do not provide GHS classification advice — your chemical safety consultant handles classification, we handle the physical label.
