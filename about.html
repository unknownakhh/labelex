01 · The Story

Founded 2008.
Built one job
at a time.

Labelex started as a single press in [VERIFY: 2008], manufacturing adhesive labels for a handful of Gauteng food brands. We grew the way industrial businesses are supposed to — slowly, deliberately, paid for by repeat clients rather than borrowed money.

Eighteen years later we're still in one factory. Still family owned. Still printing the same brands we started with — alongside a few hundred new ones. We've added equipment when we needed capacity, added substrates when clients asked, and added a second product line, then a third, when the volume justified it.

The thing that hasn't changed: every job runs through our floor. No middlemen. No outsourced production. No "we've forwarded your order to our partner facility." If your label has Labelex on the dispatch note, it was made by us.

03 · How We Work

From brief to dispatch.

Every order moves through the same four stages. Tight loop, real timelines, single point of accountability per job.

STEP 01

Brief.

You send the spec — form, WhatsApp, email, or factory visit. Artwork, quantities, container shape, deadline, substrate preference if known.

Within 24 hours you'll have a quote with pricing tiers, lead time, and any clarifying questions. No procurement department gauntlet — you speak directly to people who know the floor.

// Quote inside 24 hours
STEP 02

Proof.

Once the quote's accepted, we produce a digital proof for sign-off. Material samples on request — physical, posted to your office, free for verified businesses.

Nothing prints until you approve. Colour, copy, dimensions, finish — all confirmed in writing before a single unit runs through the press.

// Approve before production
STEP 03

Produce.

Your job runs in our Gauteng facility. Quality-checked at three stages: pre-press setup, mid-run colour and registration, dispatch-final visual sign-off.

Every order has a dedicated production manager — single point of accountability. You'll know who to call. Standard runs ship in 5–10 working days, depending on substrate availability and finish complexity.

// 5–10 working days standard
STEP 04

Dispatch.

Packed to courier spec, dispatched nationwide. All nine SA provinces. Tracking link sent on dispatch — you'll see your order move from factory to door.

Your job specs, batch info, and substrate lot are kept on file for seven years. Reorder requests reuse the original setup — no re-briefing, no re-proofing for repeat work.

// Tracked · 7-year file retention
04 · What We Stand For

Four things
we don't do.

Most label manufacturers list the things they do well. We'd rather tell you what we won't do — because that's where most label suppliers fall short.

PRINCIPLE 01

No middlemen.

We manufacture in-house from origination to dispatch. Your job runs on our floor, on our presses, by our team. No "trusted partner facility" routing. No surprise outsourcing. The factory you're quoted by is the factory that ships your order.

PRINCIPLE 02

No surprise lead times.

Quoted is delivered. We don't pad timelines and we don't ship late. If we say 5–10 working days, we mean 5–10 working days. If a substrate's out of stock or a press is booked, you'll know at quote stage — not week three.

PRINCIPLE 03

No minimum that doesn't make sense.

Our adhesive line runs from 100 units. Heat shrink from 10,000. Hot melt from 100,000. Each MOQ matches the economics of the production technology — not an arbitrary "we don't bother below this number" policy. Small launch? Talk to us about adhesive.

PRINCIPLE 04

No hiding behind departments.

You speak directly to people who know the floor. No account manager firewall, no "let me check with production." When you call, you reach someone who can answer about your job — substrate, schedule, status — without putting you on hold to ask someone else.
