01 · The Story

Built one job
at a time.

Labelex started in Gauteng producing adhesive labels for local food and FMCG brands. We grew the way businesses are supposed to — through repeat clients, consistent quality and word of mouth rather than borrowed money or aggressive sales.

Sixteen years later we are still Gauteng based. Still serving the same industries we started with alongside hundreds of new clients across every South African province. We have expanded our product range, our substrate library and our delivery reach — one justified step at a time.

What has not changed is the way we work. Every order is managed directly by our team. You deal with people who know your brief, know your product and know what needs to happen to get your labels to your door on time.

01 · Foundation
Started with adhesive sticker labels
Serving Gauteng food and FMCG brands. Coated paper and basic substrates. Built on repeat orders and direct client relationships.
02 · Expansion
Substrate range grown to 7 options
Added BOPP, synthetic paper, metallised films, kraft and luxury texture to serve cosmetics, pharmaceutical and premium brand clients.
03 · Second format
Heat shrink sleeve capability added
PETG, PVC and opaque white shrink film. Brought in to serve beverage, personal care and supplement brands needing 360 degree coverage.
04 · Third format
Hot melt glue label capability added
High volume roll-fed labelling for cylindrical containers. Added to serve FMCG and beverage producers running large scale production lines.
05 · National reach
Delivering to all nine provinces
Nationwide courier network established. Tracking on every order. Same lead time regardless of destination — 14 to 21 days from deposit to door.
06 · Today
Three formats, 13 substrates, nationwide
16 years in the label business. Minimum order R3,000. Every order quality checked and shown to the client via WhatsApp before dispatch.

can produce.

03 · How We Work

From brief to dispatch.

Every order moves through the same four steps. You know exactly where your job is from the moment you send the brief to the day it arrives at your door.

STEP 01

Brief.

Send us your artwork, quantities, what you are labelling and your deadline via the form, WhatsApp or email. We will have a quote back to you within 24 hours.

// Quote within 24 hours
STEP 02

Confirm.

We send a digital proof for your sign-off. Nothing goes into production until you confirm. A 50% deposit secures your production slot and your 14 to 21 day lead time begins from the date of payment.

// Approve before production starts
STEP 03

Produce.

Your order goes into production and is quality checked before it moves. Once complete we send you WhatsApp footage of your finished labels so you can see them before you pay the balance.

// You see your labels before paying the balance

Dispatch.

04 · What We Stand For

Four things
we commit to.

Not claims. Commitments. Every one of these is built into the way we handle every order we take on.

PRINCIPLE 01

You see your labels before you pay the balance.

Once your order is complete we send you WhatsApp footage of your finished labels before we ask for the balance payment. You confirm what you see, then we ship. No surprises on delivery. No disputes after the fact.

PRINCIPLE 02

Honest lead times. Stated upfront.

Our lead time is 14 to 21 days from the date of your deposit. We state this before you commit, not after. If anything changes that affects your timeline you will know immediately — not when your delivery is already late.

PRINCIPLE 03

One minimum across all three formats.

Minimum order value is R3,000 across adhesive sticker labels, heat shrink sleeves and hot melt glue labels. No format-specific minimums, no unit thresholds, no arbitrary policies. R3,000 gets you into production regardless of which format you need.

PRINCIPLE 04

Direct communication on every order.

When you contact us you reach someone who knows your brief. We do not route enquiries through departments or account managers. You get a real answer about your order — substrate, status, timeline — without being put on hold.
