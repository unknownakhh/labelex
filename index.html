Process

From brief to dispatch.

A transparent four-step process. You know exactly where your order is from the moment you send the brief to the day it arrives at your door.

01 · BRIEF

Send the spec.

Contact us via the form, WhatsApp or visit our Gauteng office. Share your artwork, quantities, what you are labelling and your deadline. We will have a quote back to you within 24 hours.

02 · CONFIRM

Approve and deposit.

We send a digital proof for your sign-off. Nothing goes into production until you confirm. A 50% deposit secures your production slot and your 14 to 21 day lead time begins from the date of payment.

03 · PRODUCE

We run your job.

Your order goes into production and is quality-checked before it moves. Once complete we send you WhatsApp footage of your finished labels so you can see them before you pay the balance.

04 · DISPATCH

Pay the balance and we ship.

Your balance payment releases the order. We dispatch via nationwide courier to all nine provinces and send you a tracking link on the day of dispatch.
